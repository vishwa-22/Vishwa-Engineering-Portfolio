# MATLAB – Advanced Communications

## Project Overview

Academic coursework completed during my MSc Advanced Electrical and Electronic Engineering with Management at the University of Leicester.

The coursework focused on wireless communication systems, radio propagation, cellular network modelling and atmospheric attenuation using MATLAB.

## Software and Technical Skills

- MATLAB
- Radio propagation and path-loss modelling
- GSM cellular network analysis
- Wireless communication link calculations
- Numerical simulation and result interpretation

## Coursework Activities

### Coursework 1 – Radio Propagation

Investigated line-of-sight radio communication and calculated a maximum line-of-sight distance of approximately 75.38 km.

### Coursework 2 – Propagation Models

Executed MATLAB models to investigate propagation loss under different conditions and analysed the resulting numerical outputs.

### Coursework 3 – GSM Network Analysis

Investigated GSM radio propagation and cellular network performance.

The analysis included a path-loss exponent of approximately 2.664 and a shadowing standard deviation of approximately 2.34.

### Coursework 4 – Atmospheric Attenuation

Investigated the effects of atmospheric oxygen and rainfall on communication signals.

Calculated oxygen attenuation of approximately 3.568 dB and examined rain attenuation under different geographical conditions.

## My Contribution

I worked with university-provided MATLAB code and simulation models. My responsibilities included configuring parameters, running simulations, recording outputs, interpreting numerical results and preparing coursework reports.

The supplied MATLAB code was not independently developed by me.
