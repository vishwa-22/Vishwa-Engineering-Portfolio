# MATLAB – Advanced Communications (EG7217)

**University of Leicester | MSc Advanced Electrical and Electronic Engineering with Management | Academic Coursework**

## Project Overview

This portfolio presents four individual coursework assignments completed as part of the Advanced Communications (EG7217) module at the University of Leicester.

The assignments covered wireless communication systems, radio signal propagation, indoor network coverage, GSM cellular network planning and satellite communication link analysis.

I used MATLAB and university-provided simulation models to perform engineering calculations, analyse communication performance, generate plots and interpret technical results.

## Coursework 1 – Radio Propagation and Path Loss

**Objective:** Analyse radio signal propagation and understand how frequency, distance, antenna height and environmental conditions affect received signal strength.

**Technical activities:**
- Calculated free-space path loss at 100 MHz and 1 GHz.
- Analysed the two-ray ground reflection model.
- Investigated antenna height and line-of-sight distance.
- Examined diffraction effects and received power.

**Recorded result:** Maximum line-of-sight distance of 75.38 km, based on the coursework parameters.

**Skills demonstrated:** Radio propagation | Path-loss calculations | Antenna fundamentals | MATLAB analysis

## Coursework 2 – Indoor Wireless Signal Propagation

**Objective:** Examine wireless signal attenuation in indoor environments, including the effects of distance, walls and floors.

**Technical activities:**
- Analysed distance-dependent signal loss.
- Investigated wall and floor attenuation.
- Used MATLAB to examine propagation models and perform curve fitting.
- Compared modelling results.

**Skills demonstrated:** Indoor propagation | Signal attenuation | Curve fitting | MATLAB

## Coursework 3 – GSM Cellular Network Planning

**Objective:** Analyse fundamental cellular communication concepts and examine how base-station placement and antenna settings influence network coverage.

**Technical activities:**
- Estimated the path-loss exponent.
- Examined signal-to-interference ratio (SIR).
- Studied frequency reuse and grade of service.
- Used supplied MATLAB models to simulate an urban cellular network.
- Adjusted base-station positions and antenna parameters to observe changes in simulated performance.

**Recorded results:**
- Path-loss exponent: 2.664
- Sigma: 2.34

The original report contains an inconsistent SIR calculation, so no numerical SIR result is claimed here.

**Skills demonstrated:** GSM fundamentals | Cellular network planning | Radio coverage | Network simulation | MATLAB

## Coursework 4 – Satellite Communications and Link Budget

**Objective:** Analyse satellite communication link performance by considering atmospheric losses, rain attenuation and interference.

**Technical activities:**
- Examined oxygen absorption and atmospheric attenuation.
- Calculated rain attenuation for Leicester and Buxton.
- Analysed cross-polar discrimination.
- Evaluated theoretical signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR).

**Results recorded in my coursework:**

| Parameter | Recorded result |
|---|---|
| Oxygen scale height | 6.282 km |
| Oxygen attenuation | 3.568 dB |
| Rain attenuation, Leicester | 43.7452 dB |
| Rain attenuation, Buxton | 40.2862 dB |
| Cross-polar discrimination | 20.821 dB |
| Theoretical SINR | 16.98 dB |

The SINR calculation used an assumed SNR of 20 dB and an interference assumption. It should not be interpreted as a verified result for a specific weather condition.

**Skills demonstrated:** Satellite communications | Link budgets | Atmospheric attenuation | Radio engineering calculations
