# SolidWorks – Gear Train Assembly

## Project Overview

This academic assignment was completed as part of the EG7010 Engineering Design Case Study module during my MSc Advanced Electrical and Electronic Engineering with Management at the University of Leicester.

The assignment introduced me to basic mechanical assembly concepts, gear train arrangements and engineering design visualisation using SolidWorks.

## Software and Tools

- SOLIDWORKS
- 3D CAD visualisation
- Mechanical assembly fundamentals
- Engineering drawing interpretation

## Project Activities

- Worked on an academic gear train assembly assignment using SolidWorks.
- Examined the arrangement of gears within a mechanical assembly.
- Gained experience interpreting and working with 3D mechanical components.
- Reviewed and documented the final gear train assembly.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Basic familiarity with SolidWorks
- Understanding of mechanical assembly principles
- Gear train arrangement and visualisation
- Engineering drawing interpretation
- Technical documentation

The folder contains the gear train assignment evidence, including the final assembly drawing.
