# MSc Engineering Management Project – GSK

## Project Overview

This individual research project was completed as part of my MSc Advanced Electrical and Electronic Engineering with Management at the University of Leicester.

My dissertation examined regulatory compliance, operational risk management and business challenges affecting GSK's international operations. The research focused on how organisations can manage operational risks, strengthen compliance and improve efficiency.

## Research Areas

- Regulatory compliance and international business operations
- Operational risk identification and mitigation
- Risk management strategies
- Operational efficiency
- Engineering and business management

## Research Methodology and Activities

- Conducted secondary research using published reports, academic literature and publicly available information.
- Examined regulatory and operational challenges affecting international business activities.
- Analysed approaches to managing operational risks and maintaining regulatory compliance.
- Evaluated potential strategies for improving operational efficiency.
- Prepared an individual postgraduate dissertation and academic presentation.

## Findings and Recommendations

My research highlighted the importance of effective compliance management, risk awareness and adapting operational strategies to different international markets.

The recommendations included strengthening compliance processes, developing local partnerships, improving employee training, encouraging collaboration and supporting research and development investment.

These were recommendations developed through academic research, not changes implemented at GSK.
