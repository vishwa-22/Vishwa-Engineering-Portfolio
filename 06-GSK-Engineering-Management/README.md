# MSc Engineering Management Project – GSK

## Project Overview

This research project was completed during my MSc Advanced Electrical and Electronic Engineering with Management at the University of Leicester.

The dissertation examined regulatory compliance, operational risk, risk mitigation and operational efficiency in GSK's global operations.

- Regulatory compliance
- Operational risk management
- Risk identification and mitigation
- Global business operations
- Operational efficiency
- Engineering management
