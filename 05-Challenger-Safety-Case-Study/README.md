# Space Shuttle Challenger – Engineering Safety Case Study

## Project Overview

An academic group case study completed as part of the EG7010 Engineering Design Case Study module at the University of Leicester.

The project examined the Space Shuttle Challenger disaster, focusing on engineering safety, technical failures, professional ethics and decision-making.

## Topics Covered

- Solid rocket booster O-ring seal failure
- Low-temperature launch conditions
- Engineering risk and safety
- Professional engineering ethics
- Stakeholder responsibilities
- Organisational decision-making

The report and presentation represent collaborative academic work.
